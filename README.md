# CRM Workflow System

Operational CRM system designed for lead tracking, reporting, and workflow management.

## Problem

Managing leads and operational activities across multiple stages requires a structured system for visibility, reporting, and follow-up actions.

## Solution

Designed a lightweight CRM workflow with lead management, pipeline tracking, KPI reporting, and operational dashboards.

## Key Features

- Lead management
- Pipeline tracking
- KPI reporting
- Dashboard visibility
- Weekly reporting
- Task and follow-up management
- Operational workflow tracking

## Tools Used

- ChatGPT
- AI-Assisted Workflow Design
- Google Sheets
- KPI Dashboard Design
- CRM System Design
- Business Process Optimization

## Outcome

Created a structured CRM and reporting system that improves lead visibility, operational tracking, and reporting consistency.
